SECRET FOUR — AUDIO

Ambient.wav is a quiet original synthesized loop.
Click, Place, Reveal, Win and Draw are original synthesized game cues.
All are mono PCM WAV at 22,050 Hz.

To replace a sound or the music, replace the matching WAV while preserving its .meta file.
GameAudio loads these clips by Resources path. No scene-by-scene audio setup is required.
Master, Music, Effects and Mute preferences are available in Settings.
